Page Flow

  1. From the homescreen, they are presented with categories and featured items.
  2. Under the different categories, they can add the items they are interested in, into the cart.
  3. Inside the cart, they are then directed to the payment selection screen.
  4. After the payment selection and address has been finalized, they proceed to check-out screen.
